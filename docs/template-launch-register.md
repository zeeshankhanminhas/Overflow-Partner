# Overflow Partner template launch register

Status: content review completed on 4 September 2026. This register records the approved communication purpose, audience and action behaviour. Technical preview labels remain authoritative: a document marked preview-only or legacy-preview must not be externally issued until its live data contract is complete.

## Action policy

- Secure requirements, Delivery Partner review, Delivery Partner execution and invoice messages link only to their tokenised external routes.
- Conversation-led client actions use a reply button addressed to the configured Overflow Partner reply-to mailbox.
- Workspace URLs are not sent to external recipients.
- A Delivery Partner review request is not an instruction to begin work.
- Project setup follows written quotation acceptance and satisfaction of the agreed commercial condition.

## Launch position

Email content and action behaviour are approved by policy and protected by automated checks. Preview and legacy document labels remain release blockers for those individual PDFs; this review does not misrepresent them as live-backed or safe for external issue.
